📊 Unveiling How India Supports Its Startup Ecosystem

As part of my learning journey, I analyzed datasets from data.gov.in to understand how the Government of India is actively supporting startups through schemes like the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS).

📌 What I did:
✅ Collected data from multiple official sources
✅ Cleaned and merged them into one structured sheet
✅ Built a base for a visual Excel dashboard with slicers, KPIs & charts

📊 Key Insights:

🏢 198 incubators selected under SISFS

🚀 1,740 startups supported between 2021–2023

💰 ₹21.675 Cr funding approved to startups

🥇 Karnataka received the highest funding (₹4.07 Cr)

🔝 Maharashtra supported the most startups (319)

🧩 I also explored ministry-wise tech startup support, showing Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) is actively backing innovation.
Ministries have provided total ₹580.2 Cr to tech startups

💡 Skills I practiced:

Excel PivotTables & slicers

Data cleaning & integration

Trend & comparative analysis

Visual storytelling with dashboards

✨ This project showed me how public data holds powerful stories, especially when it comes to understanding the impact of government schemes.
